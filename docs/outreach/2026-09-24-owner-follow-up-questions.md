# Follow-up questions for the owner

Messages to send the owner of Jeyo's Hardhit Fitness Center over Messenger, after the first interview. Send the intro first. Send the question list if the owner prefers to answer over Messenger, or bring it to the follow-up chat.

## Intro message

Hi po! 👋 It's Casas, the BSIT student from MSU-IIT who talked with you about the gym system for Jeyo's Hardhit. Thanks again for your time last time!

We've started building the system, and we have a few follow-up questions to make sure it fits how your gym runs. They're about the daily report, member renewals, brownouts, reminders, and the equipment at your front desk.

Would you be free for another quick 15–20 minute chat (in person or online)? If it's easier, we can also send the questions here and you can answer whenever you're free.

Thank you po! 😊

## Question list

Hi po! 👋 Here are the questions we mentioned. Short answers are fine, and feel free to skip any you're unsure about.

**Daily report**

1. At closing, we plan to show cash and GCash/Maya totals, membership and walk-in income, product and shake sales, number of check-ins, members about to expire, and low stock. Anything missing? What matters most to you?

**Memberships**

2. Early renewals add the new month to the old expiry, so Sept 30 renewed on Sept 25 becomes Oct 30. Late renewals start on the payment day. Is that how you do it now?
3. A member expiring on the 31st would expire Feb 28 in February, then go back to Mar 31 in March. Okay with you?
4. We need permission from your current logbook members to save their details and photo. Should we ask them on their next visit, or do you have another way?

**Brownouts**

5. After a brownout, staff can enter paper records from the last 24 hours. Anything older, only you can enter. Does that work?

**Reminders**

6. Should expiry reminders go by text (small cost per message) or email (free)? How many days before expiry?

**About the gym**

7. About how many active members do you have, and how many walk-ins on a normal day?
8. What are your plans and prices, walk-in rate, and shake menu with add-ons?
9. How many front desk staff do you have, and what are your opening hours?
10. Do GCash and Maya payments always go to your own account?

**Equipment**

11. Is the front desk computer a desktop or laptop, and does it run Windows?
12. Do you have a printer, or plan to get one? We'd use it for member cards and a backup member list for brownouts.
13. Do you have a spare tablet or phone for scanning member codes at the entrance?
14. Do you have your router login? We'd like to drop by once to check your Wi-Fi setup.

That's all po! Thank you so much, this really helps us build something that fits your gym. 😊

### Full message with questions

Hi [Pangalan ng may-ari]!

Salamat ulit sa oras niyo nung nakaraan. Sinimulan na po namin gawin yung system para sa Jeyo's Hardhit Fitness Center, at may ilang bagay pa kaming kailangang itanong na kayo lang po ang makakasagot. Okay lang kahit maikli ang sagot. Kung mas madali, pwede rin po nating pag-usapan sa tawag o dadaan kami sa gym.

**Sa daily report**

1. Pagsara ng gym, ano po ang gusto niyong makita? Plano naming ipakita ang total na cash at GCash/Maya, kita sa membership at walk-in, benta ng products at shakes, ilan ang pumasok, sinong members ang malapit nang ma-expire, at anong items ang paubos na. May kulang po ba? Alin dito ang pinakamahalaga sa inyo?

**Sa membership**

2. Kapag maagang nag-renew ang member, plano naming idagdag ang bagong buwan sa dati niyang expiry para walang masayang na araw. Halimbawa, kung Sept 30 ang expiry at nag-renew siya ng Sept 25, magiging Oct 30 ang bagong expiry. Kung late naman nag-renew, magsisimula ang bagong buwan sa araw ng bayad. Ganito rin po ba ang ginagawa niyo ngayon?
3. Hindi pare-pareho ang haba ng mga buwan. Kung 31 ang expiry ng member, magiging Feb 28 ito pagdating ng February, tapos babalik sa March 31 pagdating ng March. Okay lang po ba ito sa inyo?
4. Paglipat namin ng mga current members mula sa logbook papunta sa system, kailangan namin ng pahintulot nila para i-save ang details at picture nila. Paano niyo po gustong kunin ito? Tatanungin ba sila pagbalik nila sa gym, o may iba kayong paraan?

**Kapag brownout**

5. Kapag nag-brownout, isusulat muna ng staff sa papel ang check-ins at benta, tapos ilalagay sa system pagbalik ng kuryente. Plano naming payagan ang staff na maglagay ng records hanggang 24 oras pabalik. Kapag mas luma pa diyan, kayo na po ang maglalagay. Okay lang po ba ito?

**Sa reminders**

6. Pwede naming padalhan ng reminder ang members bago ma-expire ang membership nila. Text po ba ang gusto niyo (may maliit na bayad kada text) o email (libre)? Ilang araw bago ang expiry dapat ipadala ang unang reminder?

**Tungkol sa gym**

7. Mga ilan po ang active members niyo ngayon? Mga ilang walk-in ang pumapasok sa isang normal na araw?
8. Ano po ang mga membership plan at presyo niyo ngayon, magkano ang walk-in, at ano ang shake menu niyo kasama ang add-ons (saging, itlog, at iba pa)?
9. Ilan po ang staff sa front desk, at anong oras kayo nagbubukas at nagsasara?
10. Sa account niyo po ba laging napupunta ang bayad sa GCash at Maya?

**Sa gamit**

11. Anong computer po ang nasa front desk? Desktop o laptop, at Windows ba ito?
12. May printer po ba kayo, o balak bumili? Gagamitin namin ito sa member cards at sa printed na listahan ng members na gagamitin kapag brownout.
13. May extra po ba kayong tablet o cellphone na pwede naming ilagay sa entrance para i-scan ang code ng members?
14. Sino po ang nag-set up ng internet at Wi-Fi niyo, at alam niyo ba ang login ng router? Gusto sana naming dumaan minsan para i-check kung nakakapag-connect sa isa't isa ang mga device sa Wi-Fi niyo.

Salamat po ulit! Malaking tulong ang mga sagot niyo para siguradong bagay ang system sa takbo ng gym niyo.
